Completionist Map v1.1.1

Realm tears
- An opened realm tear drops off the map, and only that tear. In v1.1.0 every
  tear left the map once a Realm Tear Labor tier was completed, even tears not
  yet opened, and some opened tears stayed on it. The mod now reads each
  tear's opened state from the save (read only, as before), and tears opened
  in another area or an earlier session are known as soon as the save loads.

MY LIST
- A new map filter page that shows only the kinds of marker you pick: any of
  the mod's families, and the game's own shops, mystic gateways, boat docks,
  area entrances, fight locations, Valkyries, quests and other locations.
- On MY LIST, [Down Arrow] opens an ON/OFF list of every kind ([Cross]
  toggles, [Circle] closes). The realm tabs are put away while it is open, and
  the filter name shows how many kinds are on.
- While the map was last left on MY LIST, nearby markers follow it: only the
  families on the list get one.

Tracking up to 5 markers
- With a marker tracked, hover another: [Triangle] Add to Compass 2/5 adds it
  and keeps the others, up to 5. [Cross] Replace in Compass swaps out only the
  marker you set with [Cross] (after a load, the one tracked longest), and
  [Cross] on a tracked marker removes only that one.
- [Triangle] Clear Compass removes everything you tracked: with no marker
  under the cursor, or on a new marker when 5 are tracked. A tracked favour
  stays. With nothing tracked, [Triangle] still opens Options.
- Works for the mod's markers and the game's own (gateways, shops, docks and
  the rest), mixed as you like, with at most 2 of one kind on the compass at
  once (the game has only 2 compass icons per kind).
- Tracked markers come back after loading a save or restarting the game, and
  a collected one drops off by itself.
- While anything is tracked, nearby markers are drawn in the world only, off
  the compass bar, and a tracked family gets no nearby marker.

Remembered between game sessions
- MY LIST, whether the map was left on it, the [Up Arrow] nearby range and the
  tracked markers, in mods/completionist-map/map-preferences.txt (written by
  the mod's native DLL; Uninstall.bat removes it).

Fixes
- Odin's Raven pins: [Cross] and the map prompt now always act on the marker
  under the cursor. Before, a Raven hovered earlier could stay selected, so
  [Cross] on another marker tracked or removed that Raven, and the prompt kept
  showing its text.

Files: the map script, the Raven bridge DLL (collectible-base-dxgi.dll, now
also reading realm tears and keeping map-preferences.txt) and dxgi.dll (which
checks that DLL's hash) changed. Install.bat and Uninstall.bat now also
recognise v1.1.0's files, so Install.bat upgrades a v1.1.0 install in place,
and Uninstall.bat removes map-preferences.txt. Everything else is unchanged
from v1.1.0: art, map data, the bundled Script Loader, the installer's
safeguards and read-only completion tracking (no save edits). Steam Deck /
Linux scripts are unchanged and not tested.
